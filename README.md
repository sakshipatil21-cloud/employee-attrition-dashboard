# 📊 Employee Attrition & Retention Dashboard | Power BI

### 📌Overview 

Built an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard in Power BI to monitor employee attrition, retention, workforce demographics, and satisfaction metrics. The dashboard enables HR teams to identify attrition trends, high-risk departments, and key workforce insights through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.


### 💡Key Features


▶ KPI Summary Cards — Total Employees, Attrition Count, Retention Count, Attrition Rate (%), Average Age, and Average Monthly Income at a glance<br>
▶ Attrition Overview — Donut chart showing overall retention vs. attrition split<br>
▶ Attrition by Department — Comparative breakdown across Sales, R&D, and HR<br>
▶ Attrition by Job Role — Highlights high-risk roles such as Research Scientist and Sales Executive<br>
▶ Workforce Demographics — Employee distribution across age groups (20–50+)<br>
▶ Attrition vs. Monthly Income — Trend analysis linking compensation to turnover<br>
▶ Attrition by Years at Company — Tenure-based attrition patterns<br>
▶ Employee Satisfaction Score — Gauge visual tracking average satisfaction rating<br>
▶ Dynamic Filters/Slicers — Department, Gender, Job Role, OverTime, and Education Field for deep-dive analysis<br>

### 💼Business Value

Enabled HR stakeholders to monitor workforce health, identify high-attrition departments and job roles, analyze employee demographics, and support data-driven retention strategies through interactive reporting.

### 📈Key Insights

- Attrition Rate: 16.1%
- Research Scientists showed higher attrition than Sales Executives.
- Employees with lower monthly income exhibited relatively higher attrition.
- Employees with fewer years at the company were more likely to leave.
- HR department experienced the highest attrition among all departments. <br>
